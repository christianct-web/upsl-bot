# Pegasus — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

## Before you hit record

### Setup checklist
- [ ] Browser: Chrome, full screen, no bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B to hide)
- [ ] Close all other tabs — only Pegasus open
- [ ] Load documents_master.csv so the app is ready (don't show the upload screen)
- [ ] API key entered and working — test one query first to make sure responses come back
- [ ] Clear chat history so you start on the welcome screen with suggestions
- [ ] Browser zoom: 100% (Cmd+0 to reset)
- [ ] Notifications: Do Not Disturb ON (no Slack/WhatsApp popups mid-recording)
- [ ] Loom: face cam ON (small circle, bottom-left), 1080p, mic on
- [ ] Do a 10-second test recording to check audio levels

### Pre-stage the demo
Before recording, do a dry run of exactly these queries to make sure the API returns good responses:
1. "What FATF compliance changes happened in 2026?"
2. "Find our previous work on land acquisition"
3. Then click the "High priority" filter in the sidebar

If any response is weak, re-run it — the API will give a slightly different answer each time. Once you get clean responses, clear the chat and start fresh for the real recording.

## The script

### SCREEN 1: Welcome screen (0:00 – 0:10)

**What's on screen:** Pegasus welcome page with the four suggestion cards visible.

**What you say:**
> "This is Pegasus. It's a legal intelligence platform we built for Trinidad and Tobago law firms. Let me show you what it does in under three minutes."

**Action:** Click the suggestion card that says "What FATF compliance changes happened in 2026?"

---

### SCREEN 2: FATF query response (0:10 – 0:45)

**What's on screen:** The AI response loads with document cards — the FATF Compliance Act, the Bill, the gazette notices, and the internal advisory memo from C.B. Williams.

**What you say (while the response streams in):**
> "I asked a simple question — what FATF changes happened in 2026. Pegasus searched 117 gazette documents AND the firm's own internal memos, and returned a structured answer."

**Wait for the response to fully load, then say:**
> "Notice it found the Act, the Bill that preceded it, AND an internal advisory memo from the firm — all in one search. That's the key. Your attorneys aren't just searching public law. They're searching your firm's institutional knowledge at the same time."

**Action:** Scroll down slightly to show the document cards. Point out (verbally) the gold "Firm document" tag on the internal memo vs the blue "Gazette" tags on the public docs.

---

### SCREEN 3: Internal document search (0:45 – 1:30)

**What you say:**
> "Let me show you what that looks like with a different query."

**Action:** Click in the input bar and type: "Find our previous work on land acquisition"

**What's on screen:** The AI response should surface the internal opinion from M. Laquis about Dorado Properties alongside the 2026 gazette land acquisition orders (LN 24, 26, 33, 42, 43, 45).

**What you say:**
> "I asked for previous work on land acquisition. Pegasus found a 2024 opinion letter from the managing partner — about a client's compulsory acquisition matter — alongside six land acquisition orders from the 2026 gazette. An attorney preparing for a new land acquisition case now has both the firm's precedent AND the current legislation in one place."

**Action:** Click on the internal opinion document card to open the detail view.

**What you say:**
> "Here's the full document detail — the opinion, who wrote it, the client matter, practice areas, and the full text. Everything is searchable and cross-referenced."

**Action:** Click "Back" to return to chat.

---

### SCREEN 4: Browse and filter (1:30 – 2:15)

**What you say:**
> "Beyond the chat, Pegasus has a full document library."

**Action:** Click "All documents" in the sidebar.

**What's on screen:** The browse view showing all 122 documents (117 gazette + 5 internal), sorted by relevance.

**What you say:**
> "Every document is classified by topic, practice area, and relevance. You can filter by section —"

**Action:** Click "Acts" in the sidebar.

> "— see just the Acts —"

**Action:** Click "Internal" under Firm documents.

> "— or see just your firm's own documents."

**Action:** Click "High priority" under Alerts.

**What you say:**
> "The high-priority view surfaces the documents that matter most. These are the items that would have triggered an alert the day they were published."

---

### SCREEN 5: Close (2:15 – 2:50)

**Action:** Click back to "Research assistant" in the sidebar to return to the chat view.

**What you say:**
> "That's Pegasus. It gives your attorneys a single place to search across public law and your firm's own work product. New gazette publications are ingested and analysed automatically. Your internal documents are private and never shared."

**Brief pause, then:**
> "We're building customised deployments for T&T firms right now. If this is relevant to your practice, I'd welcome 30 minutes to show you a live demo tailored to your firm."

**Action:** Stay on the chat screen. Let Loom show your face cam for the last few seconds.

> "I'm Christian Chee Ting at Mana Labs 8. My details are in the email. Thanks for watching."

---

### SCREEN 6: End (2:50 – 3:00)

**Hold the screen for 3 seconds, then stop recording.**

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Don't narrate what you're clicking.** Don't say "now I'm going to click on this button." Just click it and talk about what it shows.
- **Don't apologise for anything.** Don't say "this is still in development" or "we're still working on the design." Present it as a finished product.
- **Don't rush.** Three minutes is plenty. Speak at a normal conversational pace. Pauses are fine.
- **Don't read from a script on screen.** Memorise the key points above. It's okay if the wording isn't exact — natural delivery beats perfect scripting.
- **Don't show the API key input or the CSV upload.** The video should start with Pegasus already loaded and ready.
- **Don't demo the browse view first.** Start with the chat — that's the "wow" moment. Browse is secondary.

---

## If something goes wrong during recording
- API gives a bad response: stop, clear chat, re-record from that point. Loom lets you trim.
- Browser freezes: stop, restart, re-record. Better to do two clean takes than one messy one.
- You stumble on words: keep going. A small stumble sounds human. Stopping and restarting sounds rehearsed.
